## Completion criterion

Partial progress does not complete the goal. Special cases, reductions to an unproved theorem-strength lemma, finite computation, and unchecked witnesses are insufficient. A disproof counts only when it proves the exact negation, including any required nonexistence certificate. If the statement or conventions are ambiguous, ask before searching; never silently solve a nearby problem.

Register the exact resolution as a persistent goal. The goal records operational liveness, not mathematical proof state. Do not mark it complete until the final result passes the full cadence below. Hardness, elapsed time, failed approaches, and theorem-strength gaps are not completion and do not by themselves justify a blocked status.

## Durable campaign state

Create before searching:

- `STATEMENT.md`: verbatim user statement, conventions, method constraints, and success criteria. Fix its revision before search; only an explicit user amendment may replace it, starting a new statement revision and invalidating earlier completion evidence.
- `CURRENT_FRONTIER.md`: derived operational summary of current state, verification queue, active lessons, next dispatches, agents, and computations. It never determines a claim label.
- `REGISTRY.md`: canonical current route and claim-label index, grouped by mechanism and terminal gap, with each exact claim, gap, smallest obstruction, next decisive test, status, and retry novelty.
- `FAILED.md`: append-only detail for every closed route or retracted revision, its exact obstruction and evidence, and what would make a retry materially new.
- `PROVED.md`: append-only history of promotions, each with its exact statement, proof or certificate, dependencies, revision identity, and audit artifacts.
- `EVIDENCE/` (or declared existing evidence roots): versioned candidate revisions, audits, computations, and certificates cited by the ledgers. Evidence files are append-only: every semantic change is a new revision-suffixed filename, a cited artifact is never edited in place, and ledgers cite revision filenames. Revision identity is the filename; do not maintain content hashes by hand in ledgers, and never track hashes of the skill, launcher, or harness (harness-generated audit metadata may record its own).
- `PROCESS_LESSONS.md`: process lessons only — rules that change how a future, materially different search is run. Mathematical claims, counterexamples, route closures, and project-specific facts belong in the ledgers, never here.

The coordinator is the sole ledger writer; workers return proofs or write only assigned evidence artifacts. Persist every mathematical work product returned in conversation as a versioned evidence artifact and index it in the applicable ledger before reuse; chat alone is never durable state. After restart or context compaction, reread the skill, `STATEMENT.md`, `CURRENT_FRONTIER.md`, actionable lessons, the registry index, and every detailed claim actually reused. The files are memory; conversation is not.

## Claim labels — literal, never inflated

- `candidate` — produced but not checked.
- `self-audited` — rechecked only by its author or producing context; for reuse this counts as candidate.
- `verifier-backed` — the exact revision passed both verification stages below.
- `promoted` — verifier-backed and recorded in `PROVED.md`; later work may cite it.
- `independently audited` — promoted and the same revision additionally passed an audit by a demonstrably different model family or independent human.

A derived claim inherits at most the weakest premise label. Route state is separate from claim confidence. At a checkpoint, a route with neither active work nor a named next decisive test is stalled and must be classified as either `(a) method failure` or `(b) evidence against the target`; “paused,” “deferred,” and “awaiting insight” are not alternatives. Use (b) only when the recorded evidence supports it. Never call a mismatched case, global compatibility assertion, or polynomial recurrence routine.

## Search discipline

The coordinator delegates essentially all route exploration, proof and counterexample construction, computation, audits, reconstructions, and evidence drafting to minimal-context subagents; it retains exact-statement control, idea gating, route registration and assignment, promotion and ledger decisions, user updates, and final synthesis. Any derivation the coordinator does perform inline is worker output like any other: it enters the ledgers as `candidate` and earns nothing from having been produced by the coordinator.

Use subagents actively, dynamically, and concurrently across diverse mechanisms, scaling to available concurrency and any explicit user, workspace, or runtime limits. Do not impose a fixed agent-count ceiling. Match fan-out to genuinely distinct mechanisms, verification needs, and finite deliverables rather than filling slots for its own sake. Begin with substantially different mechanisms and preserve independence in early exploration. If agents converge on one terminal gap, redirect the surplus.

Before substantial exploration of any coordinator-selected idea, route, reduction, imported theorem application, or materially changed retry, run a quick idea gate with a fresh minimal-context critic. Give it only the frozen target, promoted premises, the proposed mechanism, and the claimed first nontrivial implication. Require a separate verdict for each idea:

- `IDEA PASS`: the mechanism addresses the exact target, its first implication is valid under explicitly checked hypotheses, it is not merely an equivalent reformulation or theorem-strength restatement, and no tiny or degenerate obstruction was found;
- `IDEA FAIL`: return the smallest counterexample, failed theorem hypothesis, circular dependency, target mismatch, or other concrete defect;
- `IDEA REPAIR`: name the exact missing premise or narrower formulation that must be checked before exploration.

An idea-gate verdict is route triage, not a mathematical claim label, promotion, or evidence that the route will succeed. Record every gate packet and verdict; a FAIL or REPAIR on an unselected idea still informs later retry-novelty checks. Do not allow recursive subagent fan-out or broad concurrent exploration of a route before the parent mechanism receives `IDEA PASS`; after a pass, scale exploration freely within available concurrency. Independent scouts at any point may propose new mechanisms — generation is delegable labor and benefits from fresh eyes; selection is not, and stays with the coordinator — but the coordinator must gate any proposed mechanism before investing follow-up workers in it.

Before every route, materially changed retry, or variant, check `FAILED.md` and record either `no close prior route` or `closest prior route is X; this differs materially because ...`. A route ending in a lemma or oracle as strong as the original target is closed in `FAILED.md`, not “one lemma away.” Reopen it only with a named new mechanism, invariant, construction, source, witness, certificate, or scope.

Proposal generation may run on any model family; selection and gating are unchanged. When a mechanism class is closed in `FAILED.md` and further retries fail the materially-new bar, the coordinator may additionally dispatch one consult to a demonstrably different-family or strictly stronger backend, with a prompt of exactly the frozen statement plus a failed-mechanism digest; log the reply verbatim as a tagged guidance artifact. Ideas taken from a consult enter only as ordinarily gated packets and are never citable as evidence.

Never count an exact reformulation or reduction to a computational problem of essentially unchanged difficulty as progress toward resolution merely because it has a new name or syntax. A reduction may support a proof of tractability or hardness only when the target result is independently established and applicable under verified parameters, or when the mapping proves a quantified structural or complexity decrease that the target algorithm exploits. Otherwise label it explicitly as an equivalent normal form, keep the original bottleneck open, and require any downstream route to bridge all the way to an independently known polynomial algorithm or standard hard source problem.

Every exploration agent must return a proved lemma, explicit construction, counterexample/certificate, a precise failing implication with evidence, or — for a proposal packet — a set of gate-ready mechanism proposals, each with its claimed first nontrivial implication. This is a finite mathematical deliverable, not a wall-clock timebox. Do not impose a coordinator-created elapsed-time limit on proof, exploration, audit, or reconstruction work, and do not infer struggle from silence or long reasoning. Treat struggle as observable repeated failure: retrying a registered failed mechanism without the required novelty, drifting outside the assigned route after correction, returning status-only output instead of the requested deliverable, or repeatedly failing the same tool/protocol step. Reject status reports, vague optimism, and unproved global compatibility claims. When a route gives evidence against the target, devote a lane to an exact counterexample or impossibility certificate.

## Verification cadence

For every candidate result, in order:

1. Give the exact candidate revision, statement, and declared dependencies to a fresh hostile auditor instructed to refute it and return the smallest concrete gap or PASS.
2. Assemble a reconstruction bundle of only the statement, high-level key ideas, allowed sources, and promoted premises — never the proof, its stepwise paraphrase, or candidate/evidence paths. Before reconstruction begins, a fresh agent shown both the candidate and the bundle (the stage-1 auditor may serve) certifies, on record, that no bundle element — key idea or listed source — amounts to a stepwise paraphrase of the candidate argument or contains it; a too-thin bundle is safe and passes, a leaky one fails. Give the certified bundle to a different fresh no-context agent that never sees the candidate, instruct it to use only the bundle, and require an end-to-end reconstruction; a gap in the reconstruction is never repaired by consulting the candidate. Then a fresh comparison agent, shown both the finished reconstruction and the exact candidate revision, maps the reconstruction to every conclusion and declared dependency of the candidate and returns PASS or a concrete mismatch: a different valid route establishing the same conclusions from only the bundle and declared dependencies is PASS; a weaker or nearby conclusion is a mismatch; reliance on anything outside the bundle and declared dependencies is a mismatch. The comparison must use the frozen statement and the candidate's declared contract; it must not create a stronger output requirement and then fail the candidate for omitting that extra promise. Preserve the certification, reconstruction, and comparison as cited artifacts.

A candidate revision contains only content submitted for promotion; supporting notes and scratch work belong in ordinary evidence artifacts, which carry no trust. Within a candidate, two content classes divide the verification labor, and neither is exempt: any claim quantified over an unbounded domain — all inputs, every optimal solution, no algorithm exists — must be stated as an explicit theorem or lemma with its hypotheses and quantifiers exposed, and an unboundedly quantified claim asserted only in passing prose is a concrete stage-1 defect. Reconstruction and comparison owe exactly these theorem-class claims; for an existential theorem, a reconstruction establishing existence through a different valid witness is PASS. Finite, directly checkable content — a particular witness and its arithmetic, a bounded table, a preregistered computation's output — is verified outright by the stage-1 audit or by supervised computation, which reconstruction structurally cannot do; once so checked, its absence from the reconstruction is not a mismatch, provided every theorem-class claim it supports is established. The split assigns each claim to the only channel able to verify it, never to a lighter standard. The user may close the computation channel for a campaign (reasoning-only); finite directly checkable content is then verified by the stage-1 audit alone.

Only the exact verified revision becomes `verifier-backed`. Every change creates a new revision, but restart verification according to impact:

- **Load-bearing:** a counterexample exists to the old text on an allowed input, or the repair changes a hypothesis, conclusion, algorithm, certificate, imported dependency, proof implication, complexity bound, or user-required output. Invalidate both stages; rerun a fresh hostile audit and then a fresh reconstruction. Never reuse a verifier response that influenced the repair.
- **Non-load-bearing:** a typographic correction, presentation change, or explicit restatement of an output already constructed and proved by the old revision. Aggregate related corrections into one revision. Give a fresh delta auditor only the old revision, new revision, diff, and dependency list. Carry the prior stages forward only if it returns PASS that no mathematical conclusion, dependency, or required output changed; record the carry-forward and delta audit. A purely typographic diff may be recorded without another audit.

If impact is uncertain, classify it as load-bearing. A substantive FAIL from any stage stands against the revision that received it. Do not rerun a failed stage on an unchanged revision in search of a PASS. The responses to a FAIL are: a load-bearing repair addressing the reported gap, which starts a new revision under the impact rules; retraction; or a recorded rebuttal artifact refuting the exact reported gap, resubmitted with the unchanged revision to a fresh verifier as a new attempt on record. A revision whose diff is certified non-load-bearing inherits a prior FAIL exactly as it would inherit a prior PASS. Rerun an attempt only for a recorded protocol or infrastructure failure, which is never PASS. Every verification attempt and verdict stays on record. Promotion records the revision and dependency identities plus every audit, reconstruction, comparison, delta audit, and carry-forward used.

Before completion, assemble one self-contained final artifact containing its dependency and certificate closure and repeat both stages on the whole result, even if every component was promoted. Then seek a human or demonstrably different-family audit of that same revision. Record the exact request, response, revision filenames, and self-attested provenance. It is evidence only: FAIL requires repair, and infrastructure/protocol failure is not PASS. If no independent audit passes, delivery is allowed only at `promoted` status, beginning with `Status: promoted only; no independent human or different-family audit has passed.`, followed by a risk-ordered outside-referee checklist. Never simulate independence with another same-family instance.

If a promoted revision later fails, update its canonical `REGISTRY.md` label, append the retraction to `FAILED.md`, mark the old `PROVED.md` entry as historical, stop citing that revision, and identify every dependent claim. Demote or reverify dependents before reuse. A repair starts again as `candidate`.

## Checkpoint and learning loop

Checkpoint after each scope-bounded exploration or verification fan-out, each significant result, before handoff, and on pause/stop. Scope-bounded means bounded by the assigned mathematical deliverable or branch, not by elapsed time. A still-running worker does not prevent reconciliation of completed work from the same fan-out: carry its assignment forward as active, and do not interrupt it merely to close the checkpoint.

1. Stop new dispatch, harvest finished work, and account for every live agent and supervised computation.
2. Reconcile route states, exact claim labels, dependencies, audit provenance, and retractions.
3. Update `PROCESS_LESSONS.md`; each actionable lesson must change how a future fan-out, test, gate, or resource allocation is run, otherwise mark it deferred with a decisive non-tautological activation test. A mathematical claim, counterexample, or route closure is never a lesson — record it in the ledgers and state only the process change, if any. Record environment friction that wasted time. Mark lessons that transfer beyond this campaign `graduate` and surface them to the user at delivery or pause; a campaign directory is not durable cross-campaign memory.
4. Clean conservatively: repair stale references and remove only campaign-created disposable clutter; preserve proofs, audits, certificates, and user files.
5. Rewrite `CURRENT_FRONTIER.md` last with the exact unresolved/resolved state and ranked next work.

The initial resolution request remains authorization across turn boundaries, compaction, checkpoints, failed fan-outs, and ordinary tool limits until the user explicitly pauses/stops or the exact goal completes. A forced turn boundary leaves the goal active for continuation; it is not an incomplete terminal return or mathematical blocker. Every checkpoint in an authorized incomplete campaign feeds the next materially new fan-out. Failed attempts, an exhausted portfolio, unavailable computation, or an elapsed time floor are not permission to return. Honor user-specified minimum active-search time, but never treat it as an exit condition.

## Reporting, computation, and sources

Certification is not a delivery embargo. As soon as an end-to-end candidate proof, algorithm, reduction, or counterexample is durably recorded and indexed, report its mathematical conclusion promptly with its literal current label (`candidate` or `self-audited`), the smallest known unchecked obligation, and the fact that verification continues. Keep the goal active: early delivery does not promote the claim or satisfy the completion criterion. Later verification upgrades the label; a substantive FAIL requires an immediate correction, retraction, or recorded rebuttal. A clerical comparison failure that does not change the theorem or argument must be recorded and repaired, but it must not delay or obscure the already-labeled candidate result.

Do not report special cases, theorem-strength reformulations, reductions to unproved lemmas, bounded computations, or unchecked witnesses as end-to-end candidate resolutions. Report promoted lemmas that remove named dependencies, promoted minimal obstructions that close routes, and promoted stronger or simpler theorems when they materially change the frontier. Reserve “proved,” “minimal,” and “with proof” for `promoted` claims. State every reported claim's literal label.

Do not narrate every certification substep unless the user asks. Proactively notify the user when the mathematical conclusion first becomes an end-to-end candidate, when a substantive audit changes it, when its label upgrades, or when the campaign completes.

A status request may receive a concise honest answer while active search continues. A user pause or stop is the exception: cease dispatch, interrupt task agents, cancel task computations unless explicitly authorized to continue under supervision, checkpoint immediately, and report that the mathematics remains incomplete. Pause is operational state, not `blocked` or `complete`.

Use computation only for a preregistered finite domain and stopping rule yielding a small witness, certificate, or table. Before launch, give it a unique name and approach ID and record in `REGISTRY.md` its named source file, reproducible command or scheduler job, resource/time limit, output and log paths, and cancellation method; never use inline stdin. Scheduler resource and wall limits apply to computation for supervision and capacity safety; they are not proof-worker timeboxes and do not turn timeout or `UNKNOWN` into mathematical evidence. An increasing sequence of finite searches remains computation and cannot prove an unbounded theorem. Never run unsupervised detached compute.

Keep computation launch thin. The requirement is the supervision properties — bounded, cancellable, reaped, logged, never detached — with the workspace's established scheduler front door or a committed workload-owned scheduler spec as the preferred implementation; where no scheduler exists, a supervised runner with those properties is the front door. Let whichever owns the job own placement, resource caps, logs, hard walls, lifecycle, and garbage collection. The preregistration above is a launch record, not a mathematical candidate and not a reason to build a second submission, reconciliation, locking, monitoring, or certification layer. Once the finite domain, immutable workload, limits, outputs, and cancellation path are registered, launch promptly. If scheduler acceptance succeeds but local bookkeeping fails, preserve the supervised job and recover its identifiers and raw outputs from the scheduler; repair bookkeeping afterward unless continuing is unsafe. Follow the workspace language/runtime policy for any genuinely necessary orchestration rather than introducing an ad hoc shell wrapper.

Separate execution from mathematical authority. A job emits raw evidence only, and its executor may itself be a dispatched agent (a computation technician): that agent's deliverable is the raw output plus how the encoding maps to the stated definitions, and the exploration-agent deliverable rule does not apply to it. Preserve its exact stdout, stderr, scheduler identifiers, and immutable workload identity, then inspect the result after the run. Apply the hostile-audit and reconstruction cadence to a mathematical witness, certificate, table, or claimed exhaustive interpretation—not to ordinary scheduler plumbing. A post-run verifier may be required before reporting a result, but it is not a precondition for starting a safely supervised computation unless the user explicitly requests a proof-grade launch gate.

Public search is only for ordinary background and standard named theorems, not for finding a solution to this benchmark, an equivalent/paraphrased formulation, or distinctive fragments merely to declare it open. A delegated searcher inherits this scope limit, and its report is evidence of a search, not of a theorem. Verify every imported theorem against its exact hypotheses at the point of use.

These are behavioral rules, not platform-enforced capability isolation. Every audit records the supplied inputs, workspace/tool visibility, model-family provenance, and which restrictions were instructed versus platform-enforced. A no-context fork, read-only instruction, self-attestation, file mode, or colocated hash does not establish blindness, independence, or soundness. Use stronger platform enforcement if available; do not emulate it with a second proof-state system.
